What this scene tests

Prototype renderer: hand-coded canvas strokes with deterministic wobble and synthesized pencil, static and ring sounds. Dialogue is voiced with the Infinite Overture voices for Leonardo and Borges; Agnolo and the caller use stand-in voices. The scene log is the part meant to carry over into a production pipeline; the renderer exists to prove the log is sufficient to produce the scene.
