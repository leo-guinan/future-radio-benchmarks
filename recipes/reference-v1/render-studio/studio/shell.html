Leonardo frames a corner of his wall and draws a smaller Leonardo who rings a bell the studio can hear. Then he puts a picture frame on his desk and draws Little Leonardo 2 inside it, who can do nothing until he puts on the Animator outfit. Identity, role and capability are separate: the outfit is a drawing that grants a role only while it is worn. Every action, scripted or yours, goes through the same engine call and the same capability check. Each stroke is planned before it starts: the pencil approaches, touches down, follows the path, lifts and travels, and the sound is played from that same plan.

    Voices and the quill’s sounds are AI-generated (ElevenLabs): the nib loop and contact ticks are shaped live by each stroke’s speed and direction. The calligraphy is the public-domain Hershey script, drawn as a pointed-nib ribbon. The walls and lamp are carried over from The First Line as a backdrop. The desk is now a surface frame, so whatever stands on it moves with it. Everything else is world state folded from the log, and the fold re-checks every capability, so replaying the log reproduces the same permissions and denials. Acceptance tests, renderer tests and an offline audio analysis run in your browser on copies of the log. Pencil sounds are AI-generated Foley (ElevenLabs sound effects), shaped live by the stroke geometry. Debug shows frame timing, the pencil tip, stroke boundaries and the audio clock.
